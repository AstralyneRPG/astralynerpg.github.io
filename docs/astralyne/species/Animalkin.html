Animalkin

Version 1.0

Found on land, in the air, or at sea, the Animalkin are a highly diverse species of anthropomorphic animals born from the god of nature. A broad term for a wide variety of different sub-species, the Animalkin are a species that can be found in almost every corner of the world. Despite their differences, their shared creation by the same god and their shared connection to nature has led to a strong sense of kinship among the different Animalkin sub-species, leading them to refer to themselves as "kin" and to each other as "kinsfolk".
