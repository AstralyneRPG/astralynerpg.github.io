Animalkin

Version 1.0

Found on land, in the air, or at sea, the Animalkin are a highly diverse species of anthropomorphic animals born from the god of nature. A broad term for a wide variety of different sub-species, the Animalkin are a species that can be found in almost every corner of the world. Despite their differences, their shared creation by the same god and their shared connection to nature has led to a strong sense of kinship among the different Animalkin sub-species, leading them to refer to themselves as "kin" and to each other as "kinsfolk".

Animalkin Traits

  • Creature Type: Humanoid
  • Age: Animalkin as a collective typically live about a century, and reach adulthood around the age of 18.
  • Alignment: Animalkin tend towards neutral alignments.
  • Size: You may choose to make your Animalkin Small (about 2-4 feet tall) or Medium. (5-9 feet tall)
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
Due to the wide variety of sub-species that make up the Animalkin, you may choose a wide variety of traits from the following list to define what your character is like. You may spend up to 7 trait points to define your character.

    4 Trait Points

  • Natural Armor: You have a base AC of 17 not including your Dexterity modifier, but cannot wear any form of armor. You can still wield a shield and wear clothing.
  • Thick Skin: Your skin is exceptionally tough, granting you resistance to bludgeoning damage.
  • Quickness: Your have a speed of 40 feet.
  • Climber: You have a climbing speed equal to your speed.
  • Swimmer: You have a swimming speed equal to your speed.
  • Flight: You have a flying speed equal to your speed.

    • 3 Trait Points

  • Empowered Strike: When you make an unarmed strike, you deal piercing or slashing (your choice when selecting this trait) damange equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier, instead of the normal damage of an unarmed strike.
  • Glide: When you fall at least 10 feet above the ground, you can use your reaction to glide. Gliding prevents fall damage. For every 10 feet above ground you are when you glide, you may move 5 feet away from your current position.
  • Burrow: You have a burrowing speed equal to your speed.
  • Empowered Jumps: You can jump a number of feet equal to your 5 x your Dexterity modifier without provoking opportunity attacks as a bonus action.

    • 2 Trait Points

  • Powerful Build: You count as one size larger for the purpose of determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag or lift.
  • Darkvision: You have darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
  • Feral Senses: You have blindsight with a range of 60 feet.
  • Natural Camouflage: You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in natural environments.
  • Naturally Aclimated: You gain advantage on Consitution saving throws against one of the following harzards: extreme heat or extreme cold.
  • Amphibious: You can breathe air and water.
  • Mimicry: You can mimic sounds you have heard, including voices. A creaure must succeed on a Wisdom (Insight) check to recognize your mimicry as fake. The DC is 8 + Your Charisma modifier and Proficiency bonus.
  • Poison Immunity: You have immunity to poison damage and being affected by the poisoned condition.

    • 1 Trait Point

  • Keen Sight: You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
  • Keen Smell: You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
  • Keen Hearing: You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

Through your unique choice of traits, you can determine an animal that best represents your traits and use that as your Animalkin sub-species. For example, if you choose the traits of Flight, Keen Sight, and Mimicry, you could choose to be a bird Animalkin. Quickness, Keen Smell, Keen Hearing and Keen Sight could be a foxlike Animalkin.
