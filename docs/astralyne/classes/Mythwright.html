Astralyne

Mythwright

Version 1.1

Through the power of creation, Bards use their unique capabilities and charismatic nature to connect with others across the realm. To bards, creativity serves as a font of magical potential and is what separates a sapient creature from a wild beast. Throughout time, Bards have worked as one to chronicle tales of the past and secrets of the universe in their own unique ways. As such, they are valued all across the land, aiding parties and ensuring that they too may tell their own tales for the future.

You must have a Charisma score of 13 or higher to multiclass into or out of this class.

Class Features

As a Mythwright, you gain the following class features.

Hit Points

Hit Dice: 1d8 per Mythwright level

Hit Points at 1st Level: 8 + your Constitution modifier

Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per Mythwright level after 1st

Proficiencies

Armor: Light armor

Weapons: Simple weapons, longswords, rapiers , shortswords, hand crossbows, longbows

Tools: Three musical instruments of your choice

Saving Throws: Dexterity, Charisma

Skills: Choose any three skills

Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

  • (a) a rapier or (b) a longsword
  • (a) a diplomat's pack or (b) an entertainer's pack
  • Leather armor, a dagger, and a musical instrument of your choice

Spellcasting

You've learned to harness the power of creation, and through that lies your magical potential as a Mythwright. When casting a spell, refer to your subclass to determine your spellcasting focus. Otherwise your spellcasting focus is your innate force of personality and creativity.

Cantrips

You know two cantrips of your choice from the Mythwright spell list. You learn additional cantrips of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Mythwright table.

Spell Slots

The Mythwright table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of these Mythwright spells, you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest.

Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher

You know four 1st-level spells of your choice from the Mythwright spell list. The Spells Known column of the Mythwright table shows when you learn more Mythwright spells of your choice. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots.

Additionally, when you gain a Mythwright level, you can choose one of the Mythwright spells you know and replace it with another spell from the Mythwright spell list, which also must be of a level for which you have spell slots.

Spellcasting Ability

Your spellcasting ability is Charisma, as your magical ability stems from your direct personality and creative potential. You use your Charisma whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Charisma modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a Mythwright spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one.

Spell save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier

Spell attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier

Ritual Casting

You can cast any ritual spell you know as a ritual without needing material components or consuming a spell slot, if that spell has the ritual tag.

Spellcasting Focus

Your spellcasting focus differs based on your subclass. Refer to your subclass for a more defined description.

Inspire

Through performance and creation, you inspire others to achieve greatness. As a bonus action, you can use Inspire to grant one creature other than yourself within 60 feet that can see or hear you an inspiration die, which starts as a d6. Any time before long rest, the creature can roll the die and add the number on a failed abiltiy check, attack roll, or saving throw it makes. A creature can only have one inspiration die at a time.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once). You regain expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Your inspiration die changes when you reach certain level milestones in this class. The die becomes a d8 at 5th level, a d10 at 10th level, and a d12 at 15th level.

Subclass: Mythric Arcana

At the 1st level, you may choose a subclass to specialize in. Your subclass is a Mythric Arcana, a multitude of different creative forces that all serve to create and chronicle the world in unique ways. This arcana determines your spellcasting focus and the unique features you gain at certain levels in this class.

You may use the navigation buttons below to view the unique features of each Mythric Arcana.

Visualist Arcana
Wielding a brush and palette, Visualists splash color into the world around them.
Songweaver Arcana
With music and singing, Songweavers weave melodies that aid those who listen.
Duelist Arcana
Master of the blade and a daring spirit, Duelists slash their blades and treat a duel as just another performance.
Scribe Arcana
Through words and determination, Scribes influence the world and shape the narrative of their world.
Lifecarver Arcana
With chisel and hammer, Lifecarver create lifelike sculptures that one could swear just moved...
Dancer Arcana
Whether graceful or fierce, Dancers move with intention and power that lets them move around the battle and strike with precision.
Thespian Arcana
Enjoyers of a good performance, Thespians bring tales to life with acting so realistic, one could swear their stories are actually happening.
Puppeteer Arcana
Manipulating strings and mechanisms, Pupetteers control their creations with precision and artistry. Perhaps they could control even more than just their puppets.
Runesmith Arcana
Combining ancient knowledge with modern craftsmanship, Runesmiths are a unique blacksmith that imbues all forms of metalwork with the power of their runes. Able to create weapons that break the boundries of the mundane.
Culinarians Arcana
Food and drinks as a canvas, these unique Mythwrights believe food is art and drink is poetry. The consumption of both, a performance of its own. With ingredients at the ready, they create dishes that fuel the body and soul into greatness.
Liars Arcana
Wielding the guise of a Mythwright, these individuals often disliked by other Mythwrights pretend to be a part of somethin they aren't. These individuals believe that lying is in itself a form of art. After all, if one believes lies enough, they may as well become true.
Mimes Arcana
A master of mimicry, Mimes can perfectly imitate any action or expression. Even making others believe something is there when it isn't. Illusions is their speciality, and they'll leave you wondering if what you see is real or not.
