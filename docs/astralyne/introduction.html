Astralyne Introduction

Astralyne is a DnD 5e setting that takes place in a semi-futuristic world known as Du'terrad, as well as the general universe of Astralyne.

Du'terrad, also known as The Double Nexus, serves as the main planetary setting for Astralyne.

Du'terrad, The Double Nexus

After an exinction level event was prevented through a wizard's sacrifice, Du'terrad was formed with two planets, Terraprim and Dukosmis, that remain in close proximity of each other through residual magic.

These double planets now serve as the main hub world within Astralyne, with Terraprim as a hub of technology, commerce, and some of the largest cities in on the plane, and Du'kosmis as a hub of interplanetary travel, universal affairs, and tourism.

The Falling

Originally, when Du'terrad was simply Terraprim, a catastrophic event was fortold. This premonition described a large moon that had strayed orbit crashing into the planet, not only knocking the planet out of orbit, but killing millions and destroying many cities. Preparations were made, and as the time came, the people of Terraprim attemped to stop the calamity that had been known as 'The Falling'.

Despite many attempts to destroy, push, and delay the moon, it still seemed that impact was imminent. Many had lost hope, until one wizard finally stepped up. One who had studied and attuned himself to the universe's magical weave, a Soleigan known as Dukos.

Dukos used the magic weave in his body and around him to attempt and attune himself to the large mass in the sky. This was no easy feat, and by doing so, he had begun to stretch and strain his body's magic. Despite many warnings to cease, he had already known that he wouldn't be walking out alive regardless. With his final moments, he used his attunement and attatched it to the planet, the strain causing his body to snap into pure magic.

In doing so, Dukos had become a magical bond between the landmasses, causing the moon to cease its descent and become anchored to Terraprim. In honor of this sudden sacrifice, the moon was deemed a planet, to be known as Dukosmis, and the two planets to be simply known as Du'terrad.
