Classes and Subclasses

A list of every playable class in the Astralyne setting.

🔮
Arcanist
A naturally attuned spellcaster, able to uniquely manipulate the arcane with their innate potential.
 🔫
Deadeye
A master of ranged skirmishes, able to strike with precision from afar or up close.
 🩸
Hemomancer
A practitioner of dangerous blood arts, able to manipulate life and channel it into devastating power.
 👁️‍🗨️
Hexblood
A spellcaster who draws power from otherworldly beings, harnessing unique arcane capabilities.
 🛡️
Honorblade
A bearer of a sacred oath, who wields the power of their conviction and fights with purpose.
 ⚔️
Martialist
A fighter of diverse martial disciplines, able to lead and adapt to various combat situations.
 🎵
Mythwright
A master of performance and creation, able to support the party in a multitude of artistic ways.
 👊
Pugilist
A master of the martial arts, able to deliver devastating blows and weave around the battlefield.
 📖
Scholar
A learned student who uses their diverse knowledge in a multitude of ways to wield powerful magic.
 ☀️
Solarpunker
A guardian of the natural world, able to harness the power of the natural world to manifest its will.
 😺
Summoner
A wielder of both unique and familiar creatures, able to befriend and command them in battle.
 👾
Technomancer
A skilled hacker that manipulates technology to wield cyber magic and further their own beliefs.
 ⛪
Theologian
A scholar of religious doctrine, able to channel divine power and provide spiritual guidance to their allies.
 🪓
Warrior
A primal fighter, who wields strong weapons with terrifying prowess and unyielding determination.
