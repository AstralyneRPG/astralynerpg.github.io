Astralyne

Species

A list of every playable species in the Astralyne setting.

🐺
Animalkin
A very diverse collection of species of anthropomorphic animals and creatures that roam the lands of Astralyne, created by the gods of nature to roam the lands. Animalkin despite their unique diversity, are often seen as a community united as kindred spirits.
🐉
Drakonians
A proud, ancient species of humanoid dragons that are often found in the mountains of Astralyne. These draconic humanoids are prideful and carry honor in their community above all else.
⛏️
Durakai
A sturdy, short species of humanoids that are often known for their mining based societies in the sides of mountains and caves. They're resilient, hardworking, and have a fondness for alcohol.
😡
Ghorak
A large brutish species known for building robust clans and tribes in the wilds of Astralyne. Despite their often aggressive nature, they are known for their strong sense of community and can often find themseleves as mercenaries, adventurers, and even traders in the world of Astralyne.
 🌲
Hollowlings
A small species of humanoids that are found in deep forests within Astralyne. Known for their love of food and revelry, Hollowlings live joyous lives in large communities and are found exploring the realm for new sources of adventure and fun.
👨
Humans
A species of of beings that are often overlooked as average, yet are capable of great feats and have proved themselves as resourceful beings.
 ☀️
Soleiga
An ancient primordial species that live among the stars. Travellers among them are known to wander the planes of existence in search of knowledge and enlightenment or to see the far reaches of existence.
⚙️
The Axion
A society of constructs, given sentience and enlightenment by the gods of construction. These beings often roam cities, seeking purpose and meaning in their lives. Many find themselves as laborers, or believers of the divine.
